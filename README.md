## What is Kiword?

Kiword is a **voice-to-text dictation app** and **AI writing coach** purpose-built for **K-12 students and their parents**. Where most cloud AIs write the whole essay (and cost $20/month), Kiword acts as a writing scaffold: it listens to your child's ideas, cleans up their dictation, and gives structured prompts that guide them to write **in their own words**.

Built on local, **on-device AI**, Kiword also doubles as a fast **voice typing tool for parents** — draft emails, documents, and messages hands-free in any application.

- **For students (ages 6–12+):** voice homework, translate reading passages, and get essay outlines that never do the writing for them.
- **For parents:** system-wide voice typing, in-place translation and AI polish, plus a shared family account with progress reporting.
- **For every family:** one affordable license covers child and adult profiles.

## Why families choose Kiword

- **Private by design.** Core speech recognition and writing features run locally on your computer. No voice recordings are stored in a public cloud. A privacy-first approach aligned with **COPPA** and **GDPR** expectations.
- **A coach, not a ghostwriter.** The **Smart Outline Generator** turns a topic into step-by-step prompts (prewriting), while your child keeps full creative control of the final essay.
- **Voice typing everywhere.** Works system-wide in Word, Google Docs, email, and any other text field — no copy-paste needed.
- **Cleaner text instantly.** Removes filler words ("um", "uh", "like") and adds smart punctuation while keeping your meaning intact.
- **Translate and polish in place.** Select any text to translate it, or let Kiword refine your wording — useful for homework, emails, and multilingual families.
- **Dyslexia-friendly assistive technology.** Removes the physical barrier of typing so students can focus on expressing their ideas.
- **Honest pricing.** Kiword costs a small fraction of a cloud AI subscription — see current plans on the official website.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Kiword free?

Kiword starts with a free trial when you sign in — no credit card is required to try it. After the trial, a family license is a single yearly price that is far below typical cloud AI subscriptions. See current plans and pricing on [kiword.app](https://www.kiword.app/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme_faq&utm_campaign=kiword-releases).

### Does Kiword write my child's essays for them?

No. Kiword is designed as an educational writing scaffold, not a ghostwriter. Its outline generator asks guiding questions ("What happens next?", "What does the setting look like?") and offers vocabulary suggestions, but the child writes every sentence themselves — that is the core promise of the product.

### How does Kiword protect my child's privacy?

Kiword's voice recognition and processing are built to run locally on your device, so your child's dictation does not have to be uploaded to a public cloud to be transcribed. Some optional features (advanced vocabulary lookup and parent-dashboard progress reports) may use an internet connection. Read the [Privacy Policy](https://www.kiword.app/privacy?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme_privacy&utm_campaign=kiword-releases) for full details.

### Which platforms are supported?

**Windows 10 or later** (x64 and ARM64) is available today in this repository. macOS and Linux support are in active development — check the [download page](https://www.kiword.app/download?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme_platform&utm_campaign=kiword-releases) for availability.

### How do I update Kiword?

Kiword updates itself in the background. To update manually, download the newest installer from the [Releases page](https://github.com/KiwordApp/kiword-releases/releases/latest) and run it — your settings and account are preserved.

### Is Kiword suitable for students with dyslexia or learning differences?

Yes. By removing the physical barrier of typing, Kiword lets students express ideas through speech and then review clean, well-punctuated text — a widely recommended assistive approach for dyslexia and other writing challenges.

### Can I use Kiword for my own work, not just my child's homework?

Absolutely. Switch to a parent profile and use the same on-device AI for hands-free dictation, translation, and writing polish in emails and documents — the whole family benefits from a single subscription.
